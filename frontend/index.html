FAQ

Good questions

How does booking confirmation work?

After you request a booking you receive an e-mail with your reference. Our team checks cleaner availability and replies in the same thread to confirm, suggest changes or let you know if we can't make it.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Reply to your booking e-mail or send us a message with your booking reference. Our team will follow up to confirm any change.

Do I need to provide cleaning products?

Product and equipment arrangements depend on the service. Please add your preferences to the request and confirm them with our team.

What should I know before requesting a service?

Review the package inclusions and estimated price, then include any important requirements in your request. Our team will confirm availability and service details before accepting the booking.

When do I pay?

Payment arrangements will be confirmed by our team before the booking is accepted.

Ready for a spotless space?

Send a booking request and our team will follow up to confirm the details.
